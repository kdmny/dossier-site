You don't ask. Dossier brings the actions and insights.

Every morning Dossier reads what changed since yesterday and tells you the one thing worth doing, with the email, meeting or message that proves it. Dossier also tells you what's already handled, so nothing slips and nothing gets done twice.

THE ORGANIZATIONAL BRAIN

Your team's AI work should make the whole business smarter.

Your people are getting more done, faster, with AI. Without Dossier, that value ends up in scattered chat histories and inboxes, and mostly buys someone a little time back. With Dossier, it builds up in one shared brain your business owns, whether you're five people or five thousand.

↗

The value stays with the business

Every time someone uses AI to close a deal, fix a process or solve a customer's problem, Dossier keeps what they learned. Dossier connects it to everything else the team knows, synthesizes insights no one person would see, and puts the next action in front of whoever should take it.

"Priya's pricing fix for Harbor Capital cut review time in half. Use it for the other three Q4 reports."Insight · from Slack and Docs · Nov 24
⟳

Continuity when people move on

When someone leaves, the knowledge in their digital footprint usually goes with them, buried in threads nobody knows to look for. Dossier has been capturing it all along: who they talked to, what they promised, and what worked. The next person picks up where they left off.

"Jordan left in October. Her Northfield notes, the contacts there and her two open promises are ready for you."Handoff · from Email and Calendar · Nov 24
